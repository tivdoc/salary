# Travel: statutory floor and remaining source intake

This is focused AI research for the frozen May–July 2026 branch, not activation, a human attestation or a completed software change.

The [original Collective Agreements Law, 1957](https://fs.knesset.gov.il/3/law/3_lsr_208274.pdf), PDF page 3, printed page 65, section 30(a)–(b), was acquired from the Knesset and visually read. SHA-256: `011a91048027ef096a2923a66fcb1747b4a70122d1cda63b06af767e6f5f2a61`. It establishes the application of extended provisions to covered workers/employers and preserves more favorable individual or collective terms. The original act alone does not establish the complete amendment status for the release period; that source-currentness task remains separate.

The already pinned [2016 travel extension order](https://www.gov.il/BlobFolder/dynamiccollectorresultitem/extention-order-travel-expenses-2016/he/extention-order-travel-expenses-2016.pdf), SHA-256 `a38a52224c50e92fef9dcf920b53d697aca42bd923639ded8cc592d5e0c43142`, was re-read from retained source pages. Its general covered population excludes protected workshops. The amount depends on the relevant discounted fare, actual commuting days and appropriate ticket options; the daily cap is not a substitute for a missing fare. The original retained article/period and directions remain mandatory. A fresh request to the gov.il URL returned 403 in this session; the retained bytes were used and are identified as such.

Implementation recommendation: an additive general-order floor policy can keep better terms as a separate unresolved coverage question instead of asserting that no better arrangement exists. It must retain the route/ticket evidence and exclusions, identify the claim as a limited floor and never describe zero or a negative comparison as compliance with all travel obligations. The historical full-arrangement recipe and receipts must remain readable. One-direction combinations require their separately documented interpretation; this recommendation does not resolve them.

Current software distinction:

- Actual commuting-day completion is now connected to the ordinary identified answer journal, including zero, unknown, correction, period validation and source-bound replay.
- The general travel recipe still has a full-arrangement gate. A floor policy/recipe and its ordinary wiring have not yet been implemented.
- The automatic payslip adapter yields a recorded travel amount, not a tariff. No ordinary producer yet fills `fare_source_context` with a current route, tariff group and complete appropriate ticket inventory. This is a source-intake/code gap, not an absent customer payslip.
- The existing non-payslip extraction accepts attendance and contract documents. An unrelated ticket or tariff cannot be relabeled as a contract to bypass that boundary. The needed intake must preserve document kind, source period, route association, personal discount and appropriate options. A user-entered price cannot silently become an authenticated published fare.

Acceptance for the next implementation: source-bound positive fare, missing/partial ticket inventory, contradictory route, source replacement, explicit no-commute zero and independent expected/comparison outputs. Retain the signed difference and show the unassessed better-terms scope. No new model request was made for this research.
