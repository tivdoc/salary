# Expected versus recorded — P05/P08

Base 3699302f4e2831d4ff76ef18eb8d921a80462aa1, whose CI runs 34283304770/34283298202 both pass. The source-provenance foundation already has six actual DEV PostgreSQL assertions on exact 054ddaf. This addition is not deployed.

`tivdoc-source-monetary-comparison-v1` distinguishes an expected amount from the amount recorded in a document and from their signed difference. It requires a complete source calculation trace whose final RuleSpec node explicitly subtracts the recorded fact input from the expected intermediate step. The existing RuleSpec interpreter independently verifies the arithmetic; this module neither copies an entitlement into a gap field nor adds a second arithmetic evaluator.

The recorded operand must be a confirmed, non-null documented fact representing a compatible monetary component. A legal parameter, declaration without documented provenance, hourly rate or balance cannot impersonate a period payment component. Expected/recorded values are nonnegative, their currencies agree, and the signed difference preserves zero and negative results. Source snapshots, distinct parameter versions and every step remain inspectable in the underlying trace. A caller cannot alter any operand or reference and repair only the outer hash.

**This remains arithmetic evidence, not a Finding or pricing basis.** `is_finding` and `pricing_allowed` are false. A document's recorded pension contribution is not evidence of a transfer to the fund. A negative comparison does not assert that the employee owes money. The synthetic fixture compares a recorded XTS figure with that same figure plus/minus one; it is not a legal rule or an independently approved golden case. A future operative writer still needs legal activation, applicability, exact period/component scope, completeness, certainty and overlap decisions before producing an unpaid-gap result or customer amount.

Local verification: 11 new comparison tests, 57 focused tests across source provenance, comparison, persistence and historical synthetic delta; typecheck and changed-file lint. Optional `TIVDOC_SOURCE_COMPARISON_DB_PROOF=1` extends the owned source-trace proof to store/retry/read an actual comparison trace over canonical customer-confirmed facts and reconstruct the comparison from PostgreSQL JSON. It creates no customer report, finding, price or settlement. Exact DB result follows after the code checkpoint; the enclosing proof rolls back its canonical trace/run and cleans its owned cases/identities and machine session.
