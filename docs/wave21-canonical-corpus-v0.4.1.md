# Wave 2.1 canonical corpus closure V0.4.1

This change places container segmentation and source-role classification on the canonical build, search, retrieval, and temporal-resolution paths. It does not review, activate, promote, consolidate, or interpret any legal source.

The 2025 convalescence registration is bound to Gazette 3384, Chapter 7, section 24. Build accepts only pages 16–25 and requires deterministic markers on both partial boundary pages. Page 25 is cut immediately before Chapter 8. Missing markers fail closed. Existing whole-container chunk files are also filtered at runtime. Container sources without a reviewed selector, including combined gazette publications and permit attachments, remain unavailable instead of inheriting their container text.

Canonical roles are assigned to all 17 registered sources. Secondary explanatory material, official implementation/corroboration, official guidance, staged artifacts, and permits pending human legal review cannot enter operative resolution. The strict readiness report covers exact date, sector, and population combinations for minimum wage, working time, pension, travel, convalescence, vacation, and sick leave. All eight gates remain machine-readable and fail closed with `LEGAL_SOURCE_CORPUS_INCOMPLETE`.

The Working Time inventory is represented as a 20-node candidate graph. Node and edge evidence hashes are deterministic, publication date is distinct from the null unverified commencement date, all relations remain unverified, and no consolidated or current text is inferred.

Pension OCR remains derived and unregistered. Transcript review creates append-only hashed revisions preserving raw PDF, rendered page, raw OCR, and normalized text hashes; raw OCR is never overwritten. Only synthetic review decisions are accepted by this engineering contract.

Worker evidence is generated offline at `output/parallel-wave-2.1/workers/w2-canonical-corpus/` and is Git-ignored.
