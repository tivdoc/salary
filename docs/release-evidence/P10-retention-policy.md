# Privacy and retention decision — 7 September 2026

This is an AI implementation/research decision, not a human legal signature or approval.

Purpose limitation and minimizing retained data apply to the salary documents, questionnaire, contact channels, reports, processing history and service providers. The [Privacy Protection Authority's current Q&A on amendment 13](https://www.gov.il/he/pages/tikun13_qa?chapterIndex=6) describes the revised personal/sensitive data framework. Its [security-regulation Q&A](https://www.gov.il/he/pages/data_security_fqa?chapterIndex=1) expressly distinguishes a person's limited access to their own data from an organizational authorization holder. The implementation therefore authenticates and scopes every export/request independently; a valid identity never authorizes another case.

The [Authority's European transfer Q&A](https://www.gov.il/he/pages/europe_transfer?chapterIndex=8) distinguishes the EEA transfer regime from the general annual excess-data review under security regulation 2(ג). We do not assume every Israeli case falls under the special EEA rules. Company policy adopts annual review and a documented purpose/hold for material kept beyond its normal age.

Company targets: documents after 90 days, case records after 24 months of inactivity, terminal-question drafts after 30 days, orphan objects after at least seven days plus a separate seven-day deletion grace. These ages are product retention choices, not statutory salary-entitlement parameters. Current documents, retained versions, immutable analysis inputs, report evidence, active upload batches, live access tokens and recorded holds block the orphan collector. Referenced versions cannot be quietly deleted to meet a nominal age. A full case purge requires revocation, accounting separation and actual execution evidence; intake alone does not perform it.

Accounting records use the later of seven years after the relevant tax-year end or six years from the income-tax return filing. [Court-hosted material quoting bookkeeping direction 25(ג)](https://elyon1.court.gov.il/heb/tovanot_yezugiyot/255b.pdf) supports that formula; it is historical case material, not a current consolidated official statutory text. The conservative engine requires the actual filing date and retains accounting records when that evidence is absent. It does not apply that accounting horizon indiscriminately to payslip source bytes. No deletion of accounting data is enabled by this release.

The 30-day privacy-request target is the service's response target; it is not represented as a universal statutory deadline for every right. Review can restrict processing with a recorded customer-facing explanation. Completion cannot be selected without an executor's evidence. Fresh authentication within 15 minutes is required for personal data export; rolling a session does not refresh that authentication time. Changing contact/ownership and executing destructive account changes still require a separately verified identity flow.

External GA4/Meta delivery is closed because inherited scripts had neither persisted consent nor proven isolation from private navigation. Existing first-party service counters continue. No salary, contact or token is intentionally sent to those external tools by the changed runtime. Configuration alone cannot reopen them; consent and isolated activation need their own verified change. Historical provider processing cannot be retroactively declared erased.

Verification distinguishes metadata from bytes and DB from Storage. The four existing cases were inspected read-only; public evidence contains aggregate counts only, and private case IDs never enter fixtures or this repository. Full restore/RPO/RTO and production migration readiness require separate evidence.
